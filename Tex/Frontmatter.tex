面向国产处理器平台完善 Android 运行时，是基础软件生态建设中的关键一环。针对 AOSP 15 官方
主线尚未提供 LoongArch 架构 ART 执行引擎实现的问题，本文以 AOSP 15 的 Android Runtime
（ART）为对象，围绕执行引擎适配、统一证据链构建和工作负载驱动优化展开研究。

本文首先完成了 LoongArch 平台 ART 执行引擎关键模块的适配，补齐了汇编器、C++解释器、
Nterp、JIT 编译器后端、JNI 编译器、运行时快速路径以及部分 Intrinsic 支持，建立了可运行的
ART 执行环境。由此，本文不仅回答了“能否在 LoongArch 上运行 ART”的问题，也为后续性能研究
提供了必要的系统基础。

在能力构建完成后，本文面向 LoongArch64 目标设备组织统一实验流程，构建了覆盖编译、部署、
目标机回归、libcore 测试和基准测试评测的验证体系，并将进入正文的实验数据抽象为正式对比快照
（\texttt{Snapshot-A}）、统计验证快照（\texttt{Snapshot-B}）、启动期校验快照
（\texttt{Snapshot-C}）、Intrinsic 补充验证快照（\texttt{Snapshot-D}）和完整 SIMD 候选
消融快照（\texttt{Snapshot-E}），从而在避免工程日志式叙述的同时保留结果可追溯性。

在统一证据链下，本文将 JIT 进入时机与运行时数据批量搬移组织为两类代表性局部优化案例，并将
LoongArch64 最小可用 LSX 浮点 SIMD 路径作为全文主结论。在正式对比快照
（\texttt{Snapshot-A}）中，方案一在 \texttt{DaCapo/pmd} 上取得 \texttt{+3.30\%} 的单次收益，
体现出更早进入基线 JIT 对短生命周期工作负载的积极方向，但统计验证快照
（\texttt{Snapshot-B}）显示其波动仍然较大；方案二在 \texttt{pmd} 与 \texttt{mpegaudio}
上仅表现出弱正收益，更适合作为字符串密集场景的定向微优化；方案三在
\texttt{SPECjvm2008/scimark.lu.small} 上取得 \texttt{+44.40\%} 的正式对比收益，并在
\texttt{Snapshot-B} 中保持 \texttt{+45.70\%} 的稳定提升，是全文最明确、证据最强的性能优化结论。

本文说明，AOSP 15 ART 在 LoongArch 平台上的执行引擎适配是可行的；在统一实验设计、固定基线
和明确证据边界的约束下，围绕主导热点开展分层次优化，能够得到有据可依的性能收益。本文为国产
处理器平台上的 Android 运行时完善与后续优化研究提供了工程基础、实验方法和性能结论。

\keywords{LoongArch，Android Runtime，执行引擎适配，JIT 编译，性能优化}% 中文关键词

\intobmk\chapter*{Abstract}% 显示在书签但不显示在目录

Improving the Android runtime environment on domestic processor platforms is an
important part of building a complete system software ecosystem. However, AOSP
15 does not provide an official ART execution engine implementation for the
LoongArch architecture. This thesis therefore takes Android Runtime (ART) in
AOSP 15 as the research target and studies execution engine adaptation, unified
evidence construction, and workload-driven optimization for LoongArch.

First, this work completes the adaptation of key ART execution engine
components for LoongArch, including the assembler, C++ interpreter, Nterp, the
JIT compiler backend, the JNI compiler, runtime fast paths, and selected
intrinsics, thereby establishing a runnable ART execution environment. Second,
it builds a unified experimental framework on a LoongArch64 target device,
covering compilation, deployment, target-side regression tests, libcore tests,
and benchmark evaluation. To keep the thesis narrative abstract while
preserving traceability, the evidence is organized as five experiment snapshots
from \texttt{Snapshot-A} to \texttt{Snapshot-E}, corresponding to formal
comparison, statistical validation, startup-phase validation, intrinsic
ablation, and full-SIMD candidate ablation.

Under this evidence framework, the thesis treats JIT entry timing and runtime
data bulk movement as two representative local optimization cases, and
positions the minimum viable LoongArch64 LSX floating-point SIMD path as the
main conclusion. In the formal comparison snapshot (\texttt{Snapshot-A}),
Scheme 1 achieves a \texttt{+3.30\%} single-run improvement on
\texttt{DaCapo/pmd}, indicating the benefit direction of earlier baseline JIT
entry for short-lived workloads, although \texttt{Snapshot-B} still reveals
substantial variance. Scheme 2 only delivers weak positive gains on
\texttt{pmd} and \texttt{mpegaudio}, making it better characterized as a
targeted micro-optimization for string-intensive paths. Scheme 3 achieves a
\texttt{+44.40\%} formal improvement on
\texttt{SPECjvm2008/scimark.lu.small} and maintains a stable
\texttt{+45.70\%} gain in \texttt{Snapshot-B}, making it the strongest and
best-supported optimization result in this thesis.

The results demonstrate that adapting the AOSP 15 ART execution engine to the
LoongArch platform is feasible, and that layered optimizations on dominant
hotspots can produce credible performance gains under a unified experimental
design, a fixed baseline, and explicit evidence boundaries. This work provides
an engineering foundation, an experimental methodology, and performance
conclusions for further improving Android runtime support on domestic processor
platforms.
