面向国产处理器平台完善 Android 运行时，是基础软件生态建设的关键环节。针对 AOSP 15 官方版本
尚未提供 LoongArch 架构 ART 执行引擎实现的问题，本文以 AOSP 15 的 Android Runtime（ART）
为对象，围绕执行能力建立、统一实验验证和平台相关性能优化三个递进问题展开研究。

本文补齐了 LoongArch 平台上 ART 执行引擎的关键模块，包括汇编器、解释器、Nterp、JIT 编译器
后端、JNI 桥接和运行时快速路径，建立了可运行、可验证的 ART 执行环境；并构建了覆盖编译、部署、
目标机回归、libcore 测试和基准测试评测的统一实验链，用于约束后续性能比较的证据边界。

在统一实验链下，本文筛选并分析了控制流、数据流和向量化三类代表性优化问题。结果表明，JIT 进入时机
调整和运行时字符串批量搬移只在特定工作负载上呈现有限受益；而补齐 LoongArch64 最小 LSX 浮点向量
lowering 链路后，\texttt{SPECjvm2008/scimark.lu.small} 在正式对比中获得 \texttt{+44.40\%}
的相对对照版本收益，并在同日重复测量中保持 \texttt{+45.70\%} 的稳定收益。本文说明，AOSP 15 ART
在 LoongArch 平台上的适配与优化是可行的，其可信结论必须建立在统一验证链、固定对照版本和明确工作
负载边界之上。

\keywords{LoongArch，Android Runtime，执行引擎适配，JIT 编译，性能优化}% 中文关键词

\intobmk\chapter*{Abstract}% 显示在书签但不显示在目录

Improving Android runtime support on domestic processor platforms is essential
to a complete system software ecosystem. Since AOSP 15 does not provide an
official ART execution engine implementation for LoongArch, this thesis studies
three progressive questions: whether a complete ART execution engine can be
established on LoongArch, how to build a unified validation pipeline, and
which platform-aware optimizations can be justified under that pipeline.

This work adapts the key ART execution engine components for LoongArch,
including the assembler, interpreters, Nterp, the JIT compiler backend, JNI
bridging, and runtime fast paths, thereby establishing a runnable and
verifiable ART environment. It then builds a unified experimental pipeline on a
LoongArch64 target device, covering compilation, deployment, target-side
regression tests, libcore tests, and benchmark evaluation.

Under this pipeline, the thesis analyzes three representative optimization
classes: control-flow, data-flow, and vectorization. The results show that
tuning JIT entry timing and bulk movement on runtime string paths bring only
limited benefits on specific workloads, whereas completing the minimal
LoongArch64 LSX floating-point vector lowering chain delivers a
\texttt{+44.40\%} gain over the reference configuration on
\texttt{SPECjvm2008/scimark.lu.small} and maintains a stable
\texttt{+45.70\%} gain in same-day repeated measurements. These results
demonstrate that adapting and optimizing AOSP 15 ART on LoongArch is feasible,
but credible performance conclusions must be established on a unified
validation pipeline, a fixed reference configuration, and explicit workload
boundaries.
