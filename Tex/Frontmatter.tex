面向国产处理器平台完善 Android 运行时，是基础软件生态建设中的关键一环。针对 AOSP 15 官方
主线尚未提供 LoongArch 架构 ART 执行引擎实现的问题，本文以 AOSP 15 的 Android Runtime
（ART）为对象，开展执行引擎适配、正确性验证与性能优化研究。

本文完成了 LoongArch 平台 ART 执行引擎关键模块的适配，补齐了汇编器、C++解释器、Nterp、
JIT 编译器后端、JNI 编译器、运行时快速路径以及部分 Intrinsic 支持，建立了可运行的 ART 执行
环境。在此基础上，本文面向 LoongArch64 目标设备组织统一实验流程，构建了覆盖编译、部署、
目标机回归、libcore 测试和基准测试评测的验证体系，为性能结论提供一致、可重复的实验基础。

在固定基线下，本文评估了三项正式优化方案：JIT 分层热度阈值调整、运行时字符串搬运批量化，
以及 LoongArch64 LSX 浮点 SIMD 向量化。结果表明，方案一在 \texttt{DaCapo/pmd} 上取得
\texttt{+3.30\%} 的单次收益，说明更早进入基线 JIT 对短生命周期工作负载具有积极作用，但重复测量
波动仍较大；方案二在 \texttt{pmd} 与 \texttt{mpegaudio} 上仅有弱正收益，更适合作为字符串密集
场景的定向微优化；方案三在 \texttt{SPECjvm2008/scimark.lu.small} 上取得 \texttt{+44.40\%}
的正式对比收益，并在同日重复测量中保持 \texttt{+45.70\%} 的稳定提升，是全文最明确的性能优化
结论。

本文说明，AOSP 15 ART 在 LoongArch 平台上的执行引擎适配是可行的；在统一实验设计和固定基线
约束下，围绕主导热点开展定向优化，能够得到有据可依的性能收益。本文为国产处理器平台上的
Android 运行时完善与后续优化研究提供了工程基础和实验依据。

\keywords{LoongArch，Android Runtime，执行引擎适配，JIT 编译，性能优化}% 中文关键词

\intobmk\chapter*{Abstract}% 显示在书签但不显示在目录

Improving the Android runtime environment on domestic processor platforms is an
important part of building a complete system software ecosystem. However, AOSP
15 does not provide an official ART execution engine implementation for the
LoongArch architecture, leaving a critical gap in application execution support
on this platform. This thesis takes Android Runtime (ART) in AOSP 15 as the
research target and studies execution engine adaptation, experimental
validation, and performance optimization for LoongArch.

First, this work completes the adaptation of key ART execution engine
components for LoongArch, including the assembler, C++ interpreter, Nterp, the
JIT compiler backend, the JNI compiler, runtime fast paths, and selected
intrinsics, thereby establishing a runnable ART execution environment. Second,
it designs a unified experimental framework on a LoongArch64 target device,
covering compilation, deployment, target-side regression tests, libcore tests,
and benchmark evaluation, so that subsequent performance conclusions are
supported by repeatable evidence.

Under a fixed baseline, this thesis evaluates three optimization schemes on
representative workloads: a tiered JIT hotness threshold adjustment, a runtime
string bulk-copy optimization, and a LoongArch64 LSX floating-point SIMD
vectorization scheme. The formal comparison shows that Scheme 1 achieves a
\texttt{+3.30\%} single-run improvement on \texttt{DaCapo/pmd} but still
exhibits large variance under repeated measurements; Scheme 2 only delivers
weak gains on \texttt{pmd} and \texttt{mpegaudio}, and is therefore better
characterized as a targeted micro-optimization for string-intensive workloads;
Scheme 3 achieves a \texttt{+44.40\%} formal improvement on
\texttt{SPECjvm2008/scimark.lu.small} and maintains a stable
\texttt{+45.70\%} gain in same-day repeated measurements, making it the
strongest optimization result in this thesis.

The results demonstrate that adapting the AOSP 15 ART execution engine to the
LoongArch platform is feasible, and that targeted optimizations on dominant
hotspots can produce credible performance gains under a unified experimental
design and a fixed baseline. This work provides both an engineering foundation
and experimental evidence for further improving Android runtime support on
domestic processor platforms.
